## Operational lesson — channel wedge from brute-force scans

`--scan-mem-for` runs `BulkVirtRead8` at 80 commands per NPF batch. A 4 GB scan = 6.7M batches = 6.7M covert-NPF round-trips. Running multiple back-to-back hammers the HV's covert dispatch and **wedges NPT_CHANNEL_INIT** for subsequent installer invocations until reboot. Operator confirmed this at 15:05 after ~6 GB cumulative scan in one session.

**Mitigation:** scan in smaller chunks (≤512 MB), space them with 30-60s pauses, OR target narrowly via PEB.Ldr-derived module ranges (each EA DLL is typically <10 MB). Per CLAUDE.md: "Heavy work at VMEXIT head freezes PC1."
